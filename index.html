Risk disclosure

The whitepaper describes Lucky Sheep as a zero-sum game and says participants may lose part of their principal. Smart-contract, market, liquidity, execution and regulatory risks also apply. Only commit assets you can afford to lose. The whitepaper says participation is not intended for U.S. or China residents, or where crypto or gaming is prohibited.

What is Lucky Sheep?+

The whitepaper describes a decentralized last-burner game using a “burning means locking” model, a countdown and a grand prize. Custody and release rules for committed tokens need contract clarification.

Can I participate from this page?+

No. This preview has no verified contract addresses or wallet integration. The game controls are unavailable until the deployed protocol and callable functions are confirmed.

The figures reflect the latest supplied Chinese whitepaper and protocol deck. They are project statements, not independently verified on-chain data; the contract address and transaction history were not supplied.
